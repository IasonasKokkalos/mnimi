## Paired tests (exact McNemar; parity guard passed across all five arms)

**Primary (pre-specified, uncorrected) — the pre-registered null held:**

| comparison | b | c | discordant | n | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mnimi vs naive_rag (n=100) | 3 | 1 | 4 | 100 | 0.6250 |
| mnimi vs naive_rag (n=80) | 2 | 1 | 3 | 80 | 1.0000 |

Observed discordance 0.04 — below even the 0.10 the power analysis assumed,
so this pair is untestable at n=100 in either direction. Not a negative

**W3 criterion: MET.** mnimi vs no_memory is Holm-significant at alpha=0.05

(a truncated-context baseline, not a ceiling). mnimi vs oracle is dead even
(b=11, c=11) at 3.3% of full_history's fed tokens — per the
pre-registration, oracle bounds evidence availability, not retrieval
quality, and matching it is not a defect in either direction.

## Run integrity events

1. **One hard failure, cause measured.** After full_history, preflight
   refused the remaining three arms ("no resolved flash_attn line in the
   model load log"). The serve log had grown to 1,223,615 bytes; preflight
   reads the last 400,000; the only `flash_attn = enabled` line sat at byte
   16,862 — written once at first model load and never re-emitted because
   the model stayed warm. The daemon was verifiably the pinned configuration
   throughout. The refused arms had produced **no artifacts**; they were
   re-run after `ollama stop` forced a fresh load block into the log tail.
   Latent harness limitation (tail window vs n=100 log volume) — reported,
   deliberately not fixed mid-run.
2. One daemon relaunch before the sitting: the tray app had respawned and
   its unpinned daemon held port 11434; killed (respawner first), replaced
   with the manual pinned daemon, port ownership verified by pid.
3. No re-runs occurred for any other reason. No parameter, prompt,
   threshold, or config was changed at any point during the run.

## Interpretation limits (from the pre-registration)

- Absolute scores carry **judge instrument error** in addition to
  question-sampling error (measured on eace081b: 9 yes / 11 no over 20
  cache-bypassed re-gradings of a byte-identical request). No absolute
  difference smaller than that flip rate is interpretable.
- The Wilson intervals cover question-sampling error only.
- Artifacts are Tier 1 **auditable** (re-grade `predictions.jsonl` with
  `--stage judge`); "reproducible" is claimed only in the Tier 2 sense —
  given the pins and the daemon precondition, on comparable hardware.
- full_history's 12.0% is a number about a truncation policy (most-recent
  ~27k tokens under a CoN prompt), not about full history; quote it only
  with the truncation rate attached.
